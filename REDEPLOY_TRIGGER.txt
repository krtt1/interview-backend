REDEPLOY TRIGGER
================

This file is used to trigger a redeploy on Railway.
Railway watches for changes and automatically redeploys when code is pushed.

If Railway hasn't redeployed yet, you can:
1. Wait 2-5 minutes for automatic redeploy
2. Or manually trigger redeploy in Railway Dashboard:
   - Go to your Backend Service
   - Click "Redeploy" button
   - Or push a new commit to trigger automatic redeploy
